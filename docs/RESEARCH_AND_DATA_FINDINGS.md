## 1. Executive Research Summary

Morni Singh's is a boutique mountain sanctuary located in the Shivalik Hills of Haryana, positioned as Haryana's premier peaceful hill retreat. It represents the nearest authentic hill station retreat to Delhi NCR and the Chandigarh Tricity (Chandigarh, Panchkula, Mohali). The retreat is operated under the hospitality umbrella of **Pratham Hospitality Services Pvt. Ltd. (PHS)**, an established hospitality management entity founded in 2001 (based in Vasant Kunj, New Delhi).

The property merges rustic hill architecture with modern comfort, artistic wall curated paintings/photography, sweeping valley and Shivalik ridge vistas, private lawns, and a sister agricultural estate—**Morni Singh's Greens**—which provides fresh, zero-pesticide, farm-to-table organic produce (turmeric, ginger, garlic, chillies, coriander, seasonal vegetables).

## 5. Verified Brand Storytelling Strategy

Based on the verified corporate pillars, the story of Morni Singh's is built on **three authentic narrative pillars**:

1. **The Nearest Mountain Sanctuary:** Escaping the fast-paced concrete roar of Delhi NCR and the Tricity in just a few scenic hours to reach the crisp, pine-scented breeze of the Shivalik hills.
2. **The Art of Slow Living:** Not an impersonal, massive hotel complex, but an intimate, self-sufficient private villa where every wall is decorated with curated fine art, books, rustic charm, and expansive mountain vistas.
3. **From Earth to Hearth (Green Legacy):** The distinctive sister connection to Morni Singh's Greens, celebrating clean air, pristine water, and organic mountain earth.
